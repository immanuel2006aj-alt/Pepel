Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of tasks will I do?

You'll see images with distorted text – you type what you read. It's like a CAPTCHA but for AI training.

How much can I earn?

You earn $0.03 per correct label. On average, workers earn $9/hour, up to $80/day.

How do I get paid?

You can withdraw via USDT (BEP‑20) or INR (UPI). Minimum withdrawal is $10, once every 48 hours.

Is there any fee?

No fees. You receive exactly the amount you withdraw.

Can I work from mobile?

Yes! The dashboard works on any device – phone, tablet, laptop.

Do I need experience?

No. Anyone can do this – no skills required.
