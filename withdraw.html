Withdrawal Policy

📋 How to withdraw

Go to your Dashboard and click the "Withdraw" button. You'll be asked to enter the amount and choose your payment method.

  • Minimum amount: $10 (≈₹830)
  • Cooldown period: Once every 48 hours
  • Processing time: 24‑48 hours
  • Payment methods: USDT (BEP‑20) or INR (UPI)
