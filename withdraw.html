Withdrawal Policy

How to withdraw

Go to your Dashboard and click the "Withdraw" button. You'll be asked to enter the amount and choose your payment method.

Minimum amount

$10 (≈₹830) – you must have at least this balance to request a withdrawal.

Cooldown period

You can withdraw only once every 48 hours. This helps us process payments securely.

Processing time

Withdrawals are processed within 24‑48 hours from the time of request.

Payment methods

  • USDT (BEP‑20) – send to your Binance or any BEP‑20 wallet.
  • INR (UPI) – send to your Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm UPI ID.
